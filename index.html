RepMeal privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

Applies to: RepMeal for iPhone, TestFlight versions 0.1 and later.

The short version

RepMeal keeps your data on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no RepMeal server, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The developer cannot see your data unless you choose to send it.

What RepMeal stores

RepMeal stores what you put into it, on your device only:

  • Phases (cut, build, maintenance, strength) with dates and targets.
  • Workouts: exercises, sets, reps and weights.
  • Food days: calories, protein, carbohydrate and fat.
  • Body measurements, such as weigh-ins.
  • Notes you write about a phase decision.

You add this data by logging it in the app, by importing a backup file you choose, or, if you turn it on, by reading it from Apple Health (see below). RepMeal does not use your contacts, your location, your photos or your camera.

Apple Health (optional)

If you tap Connect Apple Health in RepMeal's settings, iOS asks which data RepMeal may read. RepMeal only asks to read: body weight, waist circumference, and dietary energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat. It never writes to Apple Health.

  • Data read from Apple Health is stored with your other RepMeal data on your iPhone only. It is never sent to the developer or anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or sold.
  • It is used only to show your weight pace and calories in RepMeal.
  • If you use Share my data, the file you choose to send includes the weigh-ins and food days in RepMeal, including those that came from Apple Health. You decide whether to send it.
  • You can stop access at any time in the Health app (Sharing, Apps, RepMeal) or with RepMeal's Off switch. Turning access off stops new reads; data already copied into RepMeal stays until you delete it or the app.

What RepMeal does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for a name, email or password.
  • It does not send or fetch anything over the internet. The app contains no code that talks to any server.
  • It does not include advertising or analytics tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not send your data anywhere automatically.

Sharing your data, only if you choose

A "Share my data" button saves your data as a file and opens the iPhone share sheet. You decide whether to send it, to whom, and by which app (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files). Choosing to send it to the developer helps with testing. Once you send a file, it is in the hands of the person and the app you sent it through, and their rules apply to it. If you send the developer a file, they will use it only to fix and improve RepMeal, will not share it, and will delete it when testing ends.

What Apple provides to the developer

RepMeal is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple collects and passes on to the developer some information under Apple's own terms: your TestFlight email or public-link join, the app version and device type, crash reports (if you have shared analytics with developers in iOS settings) and any feedback or screenshots you send through TestFlight. RepMeal does not add to this. You can turn off sharing in Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, and you can stop testing at any time in TestFlight. Apple's privacy policy covers its part: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data lives in RepMeal's storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it. The device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include app data under your iPhone settings; RepMeal does not control that. Because nothing is stored elsewhere by RepMeal, there is nothing for the developer to delete on a server. If you sent the developer a file, ask them to delete it at the contact below.

Children

RepMeal is for people 18 and over. Nutrition and training targets in RepMeal are not set up for under-18s. RepMeal does not ask for your age and does not collect data from anyone at all, so it does not knowingly collect children's data.

Not medical advice

RepMeal shows what you logged and the performance you recorded, for example whether your lifts held up during a cut. It does not measure muscle gain or loss and it does not give medical or dietary advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing how you eat if you have a health condition.

Planned later: iCloud sync (not in the app today)

A later version may sync your data between your own devices using your own iCloud account. This is not in any version yet. Before that ships, this policy will be updated to say what is synced and where, and the update will be dated at the top.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes. Material changes will be described in the TestFlight "What to test" notes.
